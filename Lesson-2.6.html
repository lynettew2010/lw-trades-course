✅ Pre-Trade Safety Protocol
✓

Risk Calculated Required

I have calculated my position size based on my 2% max risk rule. Stop loss is defined before entry. I know exactly how much I can lose on this trade.

✓

Plan Defined Required

I have a clear entry, stop, and target. This trade fits my strategy criteria. I'm not improvising or "seeing what happens."

✓

Mindset Check Critical

I am not trading to recover losses. I am not angry, frustrated, or FOMO-driven. I can walk away right now if this trade disappears.

✓

Market Conditions Required

I have checked the economic calendar. No high-impact news in the next 30 minutes. Spread is normal. Volume is adequate for my strategy.

✗

Revenge Trading Check STOP TRADING

I have NOT just taken a loss. I am NOT sizing up to make it back. I am NOT chasing price. If any are true, CLOSE THE PLATFORM NOW.

✓

Cushion Status Required

I know my current cushion ($2,000 max loss remaining). This trade will not breach my daily loss limit even if it hits stop immediately.
