Module 3: The Blow-Up

THE REVENGE SPIRAL

How one emotional trade triggers a cascade of catastrophic decisions

🔍 Click any phase above to see the psychology breakdown

The revenge spiral is predictable. Every blown account follows this same emotional pattern. The key is recognizing which phase you're in before it's too late.

Critical Insight: The spiral accelerates. Each loop gets faster and more destructive. By phase 4, most traders have already lost control.

1️⃣ The Initial Loss

A normal losing trade that should be accepted as part of the game. Instead, it triggers an emotional response.

2️⃣ Frustration Builds

"I can't believe that stopped me out!" The market becomes personal. You feel wronged by price action.

3️⃣ Urgency & FOMO

"I need to get this back NOW." The fear of missing the recovery move overrides patience.

4️⃣ The Oversized Trade

Doubling position size to "make it back faster." Risk management is abandoned completely.

5️⃣ Bigger Loss

The oversized trade fails. Now you're down significantly more than the original loss.

6️⃣ Desperation

"I have to recover this." Complete loss of perspective. Trading becomes gambling.

🛑 BREAK THE CYCLE

The only way out is to recognize the spiral early and execute your emergency protocol. Module 4 teaches you exactly how.
